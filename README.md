I worked on a Banking Analytics project where I analyzed customer, loan, debit-card and credit-card data
to understand customer behavior, loan portfolio performance, card usage and risk.
The main objective of the project was to help the bank make data-driven decisions related to lending,
customer engagement, risk management and cross-selling of banking products.
The project consisted of multiple datasets, including customer information, loan details, debit-card
transactions and credit-card transactions. I first understood the business requirements and identified the
grain of each dataset, primary keys, relationships and important business fields.
The first step was data cleaning and transformation. I checked for duplicate records, missing values,
inconsistent date formats, incorrect data types, invalid transaction amounts and inconsistent categorical
values. I standardized the data and prepared it for analysis.
After that, I used SQL/MySQL for data analysis. For the loan portfolio, I analyzed metrics such as total loan
amount, number of active and closed loans, delinquent loans, default loans, recovery amount,
recovery percentage and NPA percentage.
For debit-card analytics, I analyzed transaction volume, transaction value, active customers, average
transaction value and spending patterns.
For credit-card analytics, I focused on total spending, transaction trends, average transaction value,
credit utilization, outstanding balance, payment behavior and delinquency.
I then created a Power BI data model by connecting the customer and date dimensions with the loan,
debit-card and credit-card fact tables. I created DAX measures for the key business KPIs and designed an
interactive dashboard with different sections for Executive Overview, Loan Analytics and Card
Analytics.
The dashboard allowed users to analyze the business based on dimensions such as time, customer
segment, location, loan type, card type and transaction category.
From the analysis, I identified important business insights such as high-value customer segments,
high-performing transaction categories, regions with higher card activity and customer segments
with higher loan risk or delinquency.
Based on these insights, I recommended actions such as targeted cross-selling for high-value
customers, early intervention for high-risk loan customers, improving engagement with inactive
card customers and using transaction behavior to design targeted marketing campaigns.
Overall, this project gave me hands-on experience in the complete Data Analyst lifecycle —
understanding business requirements, data cleaning, SQL analysis, data modeling, DAX, Power BI
visualization and converting data into actionable business insights
